\section{Native-Protocol Competitor Reproduction}
\label{sec:native_results}
The server also contains official-checkpoint reproduction runs on the methods' native datasets. We retain their latest corrected outputs as implementation checks. These use different candidate pools, modalities and, where marked, formula or mass information; they must not be merged with Test-200. No claim of matching a published number is needed to interpret these locally reproduced measurements.
